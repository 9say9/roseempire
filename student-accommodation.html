What is the MOQ for student accommodation bedding?

Mattress protectors are 20 pieces per size (one trade box). Feather and down pillows are 5 pieces per box in value vacuum packed or premium zip bag packs. Mix singles, 4ft small doubles and doubles across separate boxes.

Which mattress sizes fit typical student hall beds?

Trade sizes: single 90×190cm, 4ft small double 120×190cm, double 135×190cm, king 150×200cm and super king 180×200cm. WQMP has a deep elasticated skirt to 40cm.

Can protectors be washed at 60°C between tenants?

Yes. WQMP and Terry (both waterproof) and QMP (non-waterproof) are all machine washable at 60°C. Stock is held at our Manchester warehouse.
