Pillow trade FAQs

Are Rose Empire wholesale pillows vacuum packed?

The value microfibre shell line is vacuum packed. The premium cotton shell line comes in a branded zip bag and is not vacuum packed. Each piece is a pair of standard 50×75cm pillows.

What is the pillow minimum order quantity?

Pillows are sold in full boxes of 5 pieces (each piece is a pair). £7.00 per piece on the value line or £8.00 on the premium line — goose or duck.

Do you have a pillow test report for procurement?

Yes. A BS EN 12935 pillow test report is available for trade buyers. Call +44 7999 988450 or request a wholesale quote.

What size are the wholesale pillows and how do I wash them?

Standard size only: 50×75cm pairs. Made to the requirements of BS5335. Machine washable at 40°C.
